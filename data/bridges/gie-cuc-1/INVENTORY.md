## 0. Headline
- The bridge is NOT continuous. Each carriageway ("don nguyen") has 3 SIMPLY SUPPORTED PC I-girder spans (3 x 25.7 m), elastomeric bearings, no continuity over piers (expansion joints per span). It cannot test the negative-influence-line / blind-ratio claim.
- It does contain genuine MOVING-TRUCK mid-span deflection time histories (200 Hz, 15 s) for spans N1, N2, N3 (Vong.xlsx). Saved as CSV here. Useful only as a weak/qualitative simply supported check of the integral invariance, with serious caveats (section 7).

## 1. Bridge type and structural system
- Source: report "2. GIOI THIEU CHUNG", sec 1.2.2; De cuong sec II.
- Cau Gie vuot song, Km213+240 QL1, in service 1998; test 2018 (report Ha Noi 2018; Drive dates Mar 2018 photos, 2019 revisions). Consultant T&D Viet Nam; client Cuc QLDB I. Test per Decision 1259/QD-TCDBVN (10/4/2018).
- Two separate carriageways (unit 1 = D1-D5, unit 2 = D6-D10; 10 girders total), 2 m median, total width 24 m (2 x 10.5 m roadway).
- Each unit: 3 SIMPLE spans, "3 x 25.75 m" (report; De cuong says 3 x 25.7 m), total L = 87.3 m (87.1 m in De cuong). Semi-precast post-tensioned RC I-girders, L = 25.7 m, height 145 cm (147 cm in sec 1.2.2), 5 girders per unit at 2.4 m spacing (Kiem toan sheet says S = 2.1 m, inconsistent), skew 77 deg, in-situ deck 200 mm, 4 diaphragms per span (ends 147x40 cm, third points 120x25 cm), asphalt 74 mm. Design load H30-XB80. Bearings: elastomeric 300x400x50 mm; piers solid narrow RC; U abutments.
- EI: from "Tinh he so phong dai" sheet: I = 0.32909 m4 (per girder, calc span 25 m), E = 31.5e6 kN/m2 (author note "thay Moi noi"); report states Ec = 33994 MPa (from f'c) for stress calc. Sec 1.2.7: girder concrete measured 55.5 MPa vs 45 design.
- Continuity: NONE. Not usable for continuous-beam claims.

## 7. Assessment for the paper
Overall usefulness: LOW to LOW-MEDIUM. Reason: simply supported (not continuous); no controlled longitudinal static influence-line series; single truck type; the only full-crossing records are uncalibrated single-sensor dynamic displacement traces.

(a) Deflection-integral invariance (integral y dt vs P*C_m / v): Medium-Low. Time histories exist for 3 spans with a nominal truck, so one could integrate over the crossing and compare N1/N2/N3 (nominally identical spans, ~5.6-6.0 Hz) for repeatability. Required processing: isolate the test-truck event(s); remove baseline drift (N1 baseline drifts 1.6 mm over 15 s, comparable to signal); low-pass the dynamic part; convert time to distance with the speed [NEED VALUE]; need truck weight and C_m (simply supported, mid-span) = 5 L^4/(384 EI) (deflection under unit UDL = area of influence line), with EI per girder x transverse distribution factor, EI uncertain: I = 0.329 m4, E 31.5-34 GPa, plus deck/composite and skew). Because deflection here seems miscalibrated and the FE model over-predicts by 30-50%, absolute BPM validation is unlikely; a relative check (integral per unit weight across N1/N2/N3) is possible.
(b) SLM with two different trucks/convoys: Low. Only one truck type; static 3-truck case is a multiple of the same truck at fixed position, not a moving ratio. Could use a ratio between the N1 event pairs only if weights known.
(c) Sign change / negative adjacent-span contribution: NOT possible (simple spans, no continuity). Note: deflection records of N1/N2/N3 are in different spans, not a continuous beam. Zero hogging seen: raw records stay one-sided (negative = downward) with no uplift of note.
(d) C_m via uniform-load equivalence: Low-Medium. For simply supported girder C_m has a closed form; the static data (3.6 mm under 82.5 t placed at mid-span over 5 girders) and Midas values could cross-check the equivalent uniform load only after the transverse distribution and axle positions are modelled. Site static tests at one longitudinal position only.

What would be needed from the authors/consultant (T&D Viet Nam / Prof. 'thay Moi', HUCE): the raw SDA-830C files and sensor calibration; truck weighing and speed for each dynamic run; Midas model; video; repeat dynamic passes at crawl speed; any continuous bridge data (this folder has none).
