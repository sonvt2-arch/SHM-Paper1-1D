# Decision letter — JCSHM, Ms. No. CSHM-D-26-00881

Source: email forwarded by the corresponding author (pasted into session, 2026-10-11).

- Journal: Journal of Civil Structural Health Monitoring
- Title: Load observability of continuous bridge beams from a single deflection sensor: invariance, blind configurations and laboratory validation
- Decision: Reject (Editor-in-Chief: Farhad Ansari)

> COMMENTS TO THE AUTHOR:
> The scope of JCSHM pertains to the shm of structures, or laboratory models with scales that support applicability to structures.

## Reading of the decision

The rejection is a scope/scale decision, not a criticism of the theory. The only experimental
evidence is a 25 x 25 x 2.03 mm steel tube with 2 x 2.5 m spans (span/depth = 100, knife-edge
supports, suspended weights). The editor does not consider that scale representative of a real
structure. A resubmission must therefore carry **field evidence on real (preferably continuous)
bridges**, or a large-scale laboratory model with explicit similitude arguments.
