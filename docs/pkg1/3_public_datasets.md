## Recommendation (top 2)

1. **NOR-BWIM / Smart Road Bridge (figshare 26880898, CC0).** Only candidate that is a field multi-span continuous box girder with controlled trucks of known (reported) weights and different axle counts, giving usable tests for (a), and multi-truck events for (b). Caveats: strain only (no deflection), a single instrumented span, and the truck GVW/axle tables live in the papers (J. Struct. Integrity Maint. 8(2), doi.org/10.1080/24705314.2023.2165606) and the dissertation (hdl.handle.net/10919/111390), not in the files. Dynamic effects on a 144 m span are low-frequency, which suits integral-based identification. Next step: extract the truck table from the dissertation, confirm sampling rate in mainSmartRoadBridgeNORBWIM.mlx, and download Strains200Hz.dat.
2. **Helmut Schmidt / Munich box girder load tests (Zenodo 20522191, CC BY 4.0), checked via its small MIT-licensed sample repo first.** It has strain and inclination during known load-test crossings; verify span layout and vehicle weights from the sample and the cited paper (Steel Construction 2023, 16, 215) before committing to the 9.7 GB download. Fallback if this fails: the Tongji lab continuous beam (request by email), and the Zenodo in-service dataset (rank 2 in table) as qualitative sign-change evidence on a three-span continuous box girder only (no weights).

No dataset found offers deflection time histories with weighed vehicles on a continuous bridge; the field evidence for the deflection channel remains a gap.
