## Notes on the choices

- **Table 9 (rows 6–7).** The dense 300-speed sweep is used because its envelope does not depend on the speed sampling, which the manuscript does not specify. The orchestrator's list named only the ≥ 10, 15 and 20 entries. The ≥ 4 entry (3.8 → 4.1) had to change with Eq. (21), and the ≥ 6 (1.7 → 1.8) and ≥ 8 (1.00 → 0.99) entries changed only because the table now prints one sweep at its own rounding. If the authors prefer to keep 1.7 and 1.00, revert these two cells; both lie within 0.1 pp of the script. The 47-speed envelope (S05 l. 80: 17.64, 7.55, 3.04, 1.29, 0.99, 0.51, 0.18, 0.11) is not used. Fig. 10 still shows the 47-speed sweep, as stated in the text.
- **Row 4 (M1).** The script gives the bias for a two-load group with its spacing changed. No interpretation reproduces the old −0.110 % (REPRODUCTION_REPORT.md, M1), so the old pair could not be kept.
- **Row 12 (M10).** The axle configurations are the ones coded in `s05_moving_force.py`; the manuscript never specified the old ones.
- **Row 1 and 13 (M2).** Δx ≤ l/20 was already the manuscript's alternative in step 9; on the single-load influence line it gives a trapezoidal bias of −0.125 % (S02 l. 22).
